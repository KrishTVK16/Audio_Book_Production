How to Prepare Your Manuscript for Narration

By Alex Morgan Jan 28, 2026 5 min read

Converting a book into audio isn't as simple as reading it out loud. Certain elements like visual charts, footnotes, and complex dialogue tags need adjustment.

1. Remove Visual References

Phrases like "see the chart below" or "as shown in the image" don't translate to audio. Rewrite these sections to be descriptive or refer to a companion PDF.

2. Simplify Dialogue Tags

In print, "he said" and "she whispered" help clarify who is speaking. In audio, a skilled narrator uses distinct character voices, making excessive tags redundant.

"A great audiobook feels like a performance, not a recitation. Giving the narrator freedom is key."

3. Pronunciation Guide

Create a list of all names, places, and made-up words with their phonetic spellings. This saves hours of back-and-forth corrections later.
