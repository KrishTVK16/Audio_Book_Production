End-to-end audio production services tailored for authors, publishers, and businesses.

01. Discovery

Casting & Auditions

Finding the perfect voice is the most critical step. We handle the entire casting process, curating a selection of our top narrators that match your genre, tone, and character requirements.

  • Custom audition scripts provided by you
  • Director-led selection advice

02. Production

Full-Service Recording

Our studios are equipped with industry-standard microphones and preamps. Whether remote or in-house, we ensure every breath and tone is captured with crystal-clear precision.

  • Neumann U87 & TLM 103 Microphones
  • Remote direction capabilities (Source-Connect)
  • Real-time monitoring options

03. Post-Production

Editing & Mastering

Raw audio is just the beginning. Our engineers meticulously edit out mistakes, breath noises, and mouth clicks, then master the audio to meet ACX, Audible, and Apple Books strict loudness standards.

  • Seamless punch-in editing
  • Noise reduction & equalization
  • RMS normalization (-20dB to -23dB)
